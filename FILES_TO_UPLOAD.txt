📋 FILES TO UPLOAD TO HOSTINGER

Here are the exact files you need to upload to your Hostinger account:

📁 FROM THE "dist" FOLDER:
✅ index.html (6.4 KB) - Your main website file
✅ assets/ (folder) - Contains all styling and functionality

📁 FROM THE ROOT FOLDER:
✅ .htaccess - Makes your website work properly

TOTAL: 3 items to upload

📍 WHERE TO UPLOAD:
Upload all files to the "public_html" folder in your Hostinger account

🎯 THAT'S IT!
Once these files are uploaded, your website will be live!
